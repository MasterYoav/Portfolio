# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two overlapping audiences: **recruiters and hiring managers** who want to evaluate technical ability quickly, and **fellow developers and collaborators** who want to gauge craft and taste. Both land on the same page. Both should leave with a clear signal: this person ships serious, well-made software. For recruiters, the outcome is a callback. For peers, it's respect and interest in the work.

## Product Purpose

An interactive, blog-style portfolio: build logs and release stories alongside the shipped applications they describe. The product is Yoav Peretz's body of work — four published apps across macOS, iOS/Android, desktop, and the web. The portfolio's job is to make that work feel real, intentional, and worth following up on. Success looks like a visitor opening a project, reading its README, and reaching out or starring the repo.

## Brand Personality

Dark · Focused · Powerful

Tool-maker energy. The kind of developer who writes clean code, ships cross-platform apps, and doesn't over-explain himself. The portfolio should feel like the software it showcases: purposeful, tight, native-quality. No performance, no showing off the build process — just the work.

## Anti-references

- **Flashy creative portfolios**: full-screen WebGL, cursor trails, scroll-hijacking, elaborate loading sequences. Style that upstages the work. The encrypted text reveal is a single deliberate personality gesture — it's the ceiling, not the floor.
- **Generic SaaS landing pages**: cream backgrounds, blue primary buttons, feature-grid templates, hero-metric sections. Anything that reads as template output.

## Design Principles

1. **Substance over spectacle.** Motion exists to reveal the work, not to perform. The EncryptedText animation is the personality anchor; everything else supports the content.
2. **Tool-maker clarity.** Every element earns its place. The code and craft of each app are the story — the interface frames them, it doesn't compete with them.
3. **Power through restraint.** Dark and minimal is not empty. Depth comes from what's precisely placed: spacing, weight, the right amount of glow.
4. **The app is the unit.** Each project should feel like something real that someone actually built and ships — not a portfolio item, but a product in its own right.
5. **Writing-first identity.** Faculty Glyphic (single Regular weight; hierarchy through size and space, never faux bold) for all prose and headings, JetBrains Mono only for dates, tags and code. Blue-black surfaces with a chartreuse signal in dark mode, true off-white with an olive signal in light mode.
6. **Keyboard and motion as craft.** ⌘K / `/` command palette, view-transition page morphs, a dock that swells toward the pointer. Every animation has a reduced-motion path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as a baseline. Reduced-motion support for all animations (the EncryptedText reveal should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`). Sufficient contrast on all text — including muted/secondary copy against the near-black background.
